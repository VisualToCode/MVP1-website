AI agent & automation

AI agents that run the work. Your team signs off.

Not a chatbot bolted onto a broken process. We redesign the work itself, then build agents that carry it end-to-end — with your people reviewing, approving, and staying in control. Real cost out of the business, real capacity back in.

How it works

Redesign the work first. Then automate it.

01

Map the work

Where the hours go, what the errors cost, and which steps a person actually needs to touch.

02

Redesign the process

We rebuild the workflow around what agents do well — not the shape a manual process happened to take.

03

Build the agents

Production-grade systems wired into your stack: CRM, billing, documents, data, third parties.

Where it lands

Cost out. Margin and capacity up.

  • Onboarding and intake — long, document-heavy processes compressed to minutes.
  • Back-office operations — data entry, reconciliation, and reporting handled end-to-end.
  • Compliance and reporting — evidence gathered, checked, and packaged for review.
  • Customer support — triage, drafting, and resolution with a human in the loop.
  • Sales and pipeline — research, qualification, and follow-up that never goes cold.
