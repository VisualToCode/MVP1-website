Case studies

Platforms we took from concept to customers.

Mobile App

Guardbay: security marketplace

The Guardbay app enables security guards to find work as per their needs at a time that suites them, apply for the shifts that are available in the areas they target and get paid seamlessly at the end of the shift. The app allows employers, venue and facility managers to publish vacant positions, find guards for a specific position, and know that available guards are vetted, qualified and will show up for the job once accepted.

Web & Mobile App

Tiny home marketplace

AI powered platform connecting designers, architects, suppliers, material manufacturers and trades with consumers building homes. A consumer searches pre-approved architect-designed tiny home designs, receives a list of materials required to build that house, is connected with trades who can build that design in their area, then project manages and pays for the entire process via the platform.

Web & Mobile App

KaGo: Uber for kids

How do we digitise the processes of parents requesting the child to be transported, child care centre scheduling the job in a system and dispatching to a safe qualified driver, and having transparency on driver location, auditability of where drivers have been, and duty of care exchanges with safety checks? We increase the safety of children.
