Two tracks — your call

Which one fits depends on how well you already know your market.

Track 1

Commercial Opportunity Assessment

For founders who aren't domain experts, or who need to raise capital early.

We pressure-test the opportunity before you spend a dollar building, and hand you the artefacts investors expect.

Track 2

Product Discovery

For domain-expert founders, confident in the opportunity.

Straight into product discovery, then develop, test, and launch — fast, because you already know the market — before moving to go-to-market and customer acquisition.

What Track 1 delivers

Nine artefacts a cheque-writer will actually read.

  • Problem & root-cause — the real problem, clearly articulated, and why it's worth solving.
  • Customer personas — who your users are, their pain points, needs, and goals.
  • Customer value model — who receives what value, and how you capture value in return.
  • Product & pricing — core product pathways and a pricing strategy that holds up.
  • Lean business model — your idea deconstructed to its key assumptions on one page.
  • Market sizing — TAM, SAM, SOM across your target geography, from desktop research.
  • Institutional-grade financial model — documented assumptions; forecasts on penetration, pricing, and budgets for sales, marketing, support, and technology; profit and valuation over 10 years.
  • Investor returns & valuation scenarios — the numbers a cheque-writer will ask for.
  • Pitch deck — the story and the evidence, ready to present.

What happens after

From an idea to a software business with a plan you can bank.

Once the opportunity is proven, we move into product discovery, then develop, test, and launch — quickly and on solid foundations — before going to market.
