BlackVault™

Your private AI infrastructure. Ownership. Control. Compliance.

Public AI platforms mean your client data crosses into someone else's infrastructure. For regulated, IP-heavy, or reputation-sensitive businesses, that's not a risk worth taking. BlackVault™ is our enterprise and government venture — private AI infrastructure, built inside your own environment.

Why BlackVault™ exists

Five reasons "just use ChatGPT" doesn't hold up.

  • Data exposure — sensitive information leaving your infrastructure the moment it hits a public model
  • Compliance — no audit trail you can hand a regulator or a customer
  • IP erosion — your prompts, workflows, and data training someone else's model
  • Vendor lock-in — dependent on a platform you don't control and can't inspect
  • Ownership — no clear answer to "where does our data actually live?"

What is BlackVault™?

Your AI engine room, built inside your four walls.

BlackVault™ runs on your own cloud account, not ours and not a public vendor's. You get the capability of modern AI — without your data, your prompts, or your IP ever leaving infrastructure you control.

Who this is for

NDIS & healthcare

Providers handling participant and patient data under strict confidentiality obligations.

Financial services

Firms that need a defensible answer when a regulator asks where client data goes.

Legal & IP-heavy organisations

Teams that can't risk privileged or proprietary information touching a third-party model.
