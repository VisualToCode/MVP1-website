Careers

Build with us.

We're a product engineering and AI automation team working with founders who've earned the right to be impatient with fluff. We hire the same way we build — deliberately, not on a rolling job board.

What we look for

People who ship, and own what they build.

  • You'd rather show working software than talk about a plan for one
  • You're comfortable with ambiguity — clients come to us mid-problem, not with a spec
  • You treat a client's codebase and data like it's your own
  • You give a straight answer, even when it's not the one someone wants to hear

How we work

Direct, accountable, and fast — without the chaos.

No vague "almost done" updates. No committees for every decision. We move at the pace our clients need, with the discipline to not break things while we do it.

Right now

No open roles listed. Introduce yourself anyway.

We don't run a rolling job board, and we won't list a role just to build a pipeline. If you think you'd be a strong fit for how we work, get in touch directly and tell us why.
