Pricing

What it costs to work with us.

Not a rate card. Not an hourly rate that turns into a blank cheque. Every engagement is scoped first, priced second, so you know what you are getting, what it costs, and when it ships before you commit.

How we price

Three engagement models. One rule: fixed scope, not open-ended hours.

01

Version 1.0 build

A fixed-price build to get your product to market, scoped against your budget and timeline before work starts.

02

Platform stabilisation & scale

A sequenced, priced plan to fix what's breaking and re-architect for growth, reassessed as we learn more, never open-ended.

03

Ongoing AI & automation retainer

A monthly retainer for teams that want a product engine running continuously, not a one-off project.

Why not a price list

The variance is real, and pretending otherwise costs you more.

A Version 1.0 for a two-sided marketplace costs differently to a workflow tool for internal ops. What we can promise is a fixed price after a scoping conversation, not a number pulled from a generic rate card that has nothing to do with your build.

Always included

What you get regardless of engagement size.

  • A fixed price, confirmed after scoping, not an estimate that grows
  • Clear milestones you can track against, not "almost done"
  • Full ownership of your source code and IP, from day one
  • No vendor lock-in, your codebase, your call on what happens next
