Platform stabilisation

Your platform is buckling. We stabilise it, without stopping the business.

Maybe it was vibe-coded. Maybe an agency built it. Maybe it just grew faster than its foundations. Now it's slow, fragile, or falling behind demand. We come in, stabilise it, and re-architect for scale, while it keeps running and serving customers.

Signs you need this

Five symptoms of foundations that stopped holding.

  • Performance drops or outages as usage grows
  • Every new feature breaks two old ones
  • Security and compliance gaps you can't fully see
  • A codebase only one person understands, or no one does
  • An MVP that got you to customers but can't get you to scale

Built with AI?

Prototypes from ChatGPT, Claude, Lovable, Manus or Replit.

If an AI platform built it and you cannot deploy, secure or scale it, there is a shorter path than a full stabilisation engagement.

Common questions

Will stabilising disrupt my current customers?

No. The whole point is that the business keeps running while we work. We stabilise and re-architect behind the scenes, in sequenced stages, so customers keep using the product without interruption.

I built my product with ChatGPT, Claude, Lovable or Replit. Is that a problem?

Not a problem, just a different starting point. AI-built prototypes usually need auditing, security hardening, and a proper data model before they can carry real users. See vibe-coded to production for that specific path.

How long does a typical engagement take?

It depends on what the technical audit finds. We scope a sequenced stabilisation plan first, so you know what gets fixed, in what order, and roughly what it costs, before committing to the full re-architecture.
