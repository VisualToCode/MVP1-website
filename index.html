Common questions

Before you book a call.

What happens on a discovery call?

Start with a 15-minute fit call, a short, direct conversation to see whether we're the right partner. No pitch. If it fits, we go deep in a 60-minute discovery call on the need, the goal, and the pathways.

What are the signs my platform needs stabilising?

Performance drops or outages as usage grows, every new feature breaks two old ones, security and compliance gaps you can't fully see, a codebase only one person understands (or no one does), and an MVP that got you to customers but can't get you to scale.

Do we own the IP MVP1 builds?

Yes. Code, IP and assets are yours. We build it that way because owned assets don't just run cleaner, they lift your company's valuation, and that only works if you actually hold the assets.

How does pricing work?

Every engagement is scoped first and priced second. Not a rate card, not an hourly rate that turns into a blank cheque. You know what you're getting, what it costs, and when it ships, before you commit.

I only have an idea, not a product yet. Is that too early for you?

No, but it changes where you start. Our Accelerate Program de-risks the idea and gets it investment-ready first, so when you do build, you build the right thing instead of guessing.

What's the actual difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers. It's a conversational interface that responds inside a bounded set of interactions. An agent acts: it takes a goal, plans, calls other systems, checks the result, and carries a job through to done. Picking the wrong one for the job either underperforms or costs you governance overhead you didn't budget for.
